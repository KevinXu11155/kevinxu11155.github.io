Abstract

Fast and Faithful Coarse-to-Fine 3D Generation

Recent 3D generative models can produce meshes with complex structures and fine geometric details, with broad applications in games, film, and digital design. However, high-quality generation typically relies on long multi-step sampling processes that repeatedly evaluate large networks, leading to long inference times. At the same time, reference-driven tasks such as game-asset reconstruction and product digitization demand close correspondence to the input image: generated geometry must faithfully reconstruct the specific reference object. Acceleration must therefore reduce sampling computation while preserving the model's ability to reconstruct that object. To enable fast and faithful 3D generation, we introduce Spe3DUp, a coarse-to-fine framework based on Faithful Trajectory Distillation (FTD). FTD directly supervises few-step students with the teacher's input-conditioned denoising trajectories to preserve its generation behavior. We apply FTD separately to coarse geometry generation and geometric refinement, compressing both sampling processes while maintaining correspondence to the reference input. We further accelerate the complete mesh-generation pipeline with efficient geometry decoding and parallel remeshing. Experiments show that Spe3DUp maintains geometric quality and input correspondence close to those of the teacher, accelerates the coarse and refinement DiTs by 10.0× and 19.9×, respectively, and achieves a 7.7× end-to-end speedup over the original teacher pipeline.
